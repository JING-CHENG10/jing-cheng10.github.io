About

I am an assistant professor at Peking University. I study how changes in agriculture, land use, and energy systems affect emissions, air quality, and human health.

Before joining Peking University, I was a postdoctoral scholar with Stanford University’s Sustainable Solutions Lab. PKU faculty directory

Research

  • Agriculture and land systems

    Emissions mitigation, resilience under climate change, and coordinated management across air, water, and soil.

  • Energy and land-use transitions

    Integrated assessment of how low-emissions pathways reshape resource use, trade, and energy security.

  • Climate, air quality, and health

    How climate and clean-air policies alter pollution exposure and its health impacts, and how those benefits are distributed.
