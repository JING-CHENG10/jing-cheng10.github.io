About

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Environmental Management at Peking University, where I advise doctoral students. My research asks how changes in agriculture, land use, and energy systems shape emissions, air quality, and human health.

My group studies how carbon, reactive nitrogen, and reactive organic compounds cycle through soils, vegetation, the atmosphere, and water. We use models of these processes to evaluate how agricultural management can reduce emissions and pollution, and how food systems can adapt to a changing climate.

Research

  • Carbon and pollution across agricultural landscapes

    Study how soils, vegetation, air, and water exchange carbon and pollutants, and how these processes respond to management and climate change.

  • Food-system resilience and decarbonization

    Assess risks from climate extremes and identify ways to cut emissions across the food supply chain while protecting production. Evaluate trade-offs across climate, environmental, health, and food-system goals.

  • Land-use transitions, resource constraints, and wildfire risk

    Develop integrated land-use assessment models to examine how climate and land-use change shape wildfire risk, and how energy infrastructure, mining, and carbon removal affect land and ecosystems.

  • Climate mitigation, air quality, and health

    Quantify the effects of climate and clean-air policies on emissions and pollution exposure, and identify who benefits from those changes.

研究方向：农地系统跨介质过程与可持续转型

围绕农地系统这一关键人地界面，研究反应性氮、活性有机物与碳元素在土壤、植被、大气和水体之间的迁移、转化与反馈机制，解析碳中和与深度减排背景下农地系统污染与碳源汇演变规律，构建面向国家污染治理需求的跨介质协同调控路径。

  1. 农地系统污染与碳源汇过程演变及界面机制
  2. 农地系统水–土–气跨介质耦合机制
  3. 土地利用综合评估模型开发
  4. 农地系统全链条深度减排与可持续转型路径
  5. 极端气候下农食系统风险评估与韧性路径
  6. 农地系统多目标跨介质协同调控与综合效益评价
  7. 气候与土地利用变化下野火风险的预测、评估及应对
  8. 能源转型中的土地资源约束与生态环境效应（能源用地、矿山、CDR）
